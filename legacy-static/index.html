How OrderFlow works

Four steps, from the first message to a paid, delivered, recorded order.

  1. Share your storefront

    One link in your bio, posts and replies. Your full catalogue, always current.

  2. Orders arrive structured

    Product, quantity, address, phone, payment method. No screenshots to decipher.

  3. Confirm, pack, dispatch

    Stock moves by itself. Print the invoice, hand the parcel to your courier.

  4. Get paid, keep the record

    Receipt to the customer, payment recorded, sale in your reports and analytics.

COD Reliability Score

Stop paying for parcels that come back.

When the same customer refuses cash-on-delivery again and again, you pay the courier both ways. OrderFlow keeps that history for you and quietly adjusts what that customer can choose at checkout.

  • Delivered and collected orders build reliability.
  • Repeated refusals and returns reduce it.
  • If COD becomes unavailable, the customer still has three ways to buy.

Reliability is calculated inside your own business only. Vendly does not share customer scores between businesses.
